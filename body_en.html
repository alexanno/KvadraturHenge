What is this?

Kvadraturen is the historic grid of streets in the centre of Kristiansand, Norway, laid out in 1641 by King Christian IV. Like Manhattanhenge in New York, the sun sometimes lines up with the streets. For every moment of the year we calculate how much of the street network the sun points straight along, and look for the moment it shines down all the streets at once. Drag the sliders to pick a date and time, or tap a yellow time marker to see when to look.
